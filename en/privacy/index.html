POROG 13

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026. Developer / copyright holder: Vladimir Mitryukov. Privacy contact: businessjuss@gmail.com.

Game data

Porog 13 is a single-player game. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number or payment details to play. The game has no advertisements, in-app purchases, advertising tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not send gameplay data to third-party AI services.

Your current scene, choices and progress are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. Text, volume and haptic settings are also stored locally. This data is used to resume your game and keep your preferences.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, the app uses Apple’s key-value store to synchronize your progression profile across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The profile includes unlocked endings, archive entries, read-page markers, unlocked additional scenes and a cycle counter. The exact position of your current playthrough is not synchronized this way.

This data is stored in your personal iCloud. The developer has no server, access dashboard for these saves or feature for reading other users’ profiles. iCloud is also governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy: Apple’s privacy policy.

Without iCloud, core gameplay works locally and does not require an internet connection.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, the recipient receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. They are used to respond and investigate your issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or other information that is not needed to resolve your request.

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and related questions, then deleted unless further retention is required by law. You can request deletion of information provided in correspondence by contacting the email address above.

Storage and deletion

Saved progress and settings remain on your device while the app uses them. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app while keeping documents is a different action and may preserve them. System backups and your synchronized iCloud profile may be retained separately. Deleting the app alone does not guarantee deletion of your cloud profile.

Cloud data is associated with your Apple Account. Available iCloud and backup settings are managed through Apple’s tools. The developer cannot delete the contents of your personal iCloud on your behalf. If you have questions about deletion, contact support; we will explain what data the game uses without requesting access to your account.

Changes

If data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Please send questions to businessjuss@gmail.com.
