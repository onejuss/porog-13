Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

Yes. Version 1.0 has no advertisements or in-app purchases. The game’s story and interface are in Russian; this English support page does not mean that the game is translated into English.

Do I need an internet connection?

Core gameplay works offline. The app includes all story text, artwork, music and effects. An internet connection is needed to synchronize your profile through iCloud.

How do I continue my game?

Progress is saved automatically. Select the continue button in the main menu. Your current scene is stored on this device; unlocked endings and other profile markers may sync through iCloud.

Why can’t I hear any sound?

Check your device volume and Silent mode, then the music and effects sliders in the game. Sound previews are available in settings. Tap and page-turn sounds are enabled separately.

How do I test haptics?

Enable haptics in the game settings and select «Проверить вибрацию» (Test haptics). On a supported iPhone, you should feel two short pulses. Also check your device’s vibration settings.

Can I increase the text size?

Yes. Choose the text size and reveal speed in settings. «Сразу целиком» (Show immediately) disables gradual text reveal.

Why is the opening dark?

In some opening scenes, the protagonist has not opened his eyes yet. This is part of the story. Text and the control for advancing should still be available.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later. Haptic feedback is available only on supported hardware.

Your data

Saved progress stays on your device. Your progression profile may sync through your personal iCloud. Read the privacy policy for details.
